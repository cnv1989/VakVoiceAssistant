# Twilio Toll-Free Verification

Twilio requires toll-free numbers used for SMS/WhatsApp to go through a
one-time verification before they can send at volume. This is a template
for the two-step form Twilio asks for — fill in your own business details
when you submit.

## Step 1/2 — Business and Contact Information

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Business Profile | Your Twilio Business Profile ID |
| Legal Entity Name | Your legal entity or DBA name |
| Website URL | https://your-domain.example.com |
| Business Type | Sole Proprietor / LLC / Corporation / etc. |
| Business DBA | Your "doing business as" name |
| Business Address | Your registered business address |
| Contact | Name / email / phone of the person Twilio can reach |

## Step 2/2 — Messaging Use Case

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Estimated Monthly Volume | Your expected SMS volume |
| Opt-In Type | e.g. Web Form |
| Use Case Categories | e.g. Customer Care, Account Notifications |
| Proof of Consent URL | A page showing how customers opt in |
| Email for Notifications | Your ops/admin email |
| Privacy Policy URL | https://your-domain.example.com/privacy |
| Terms & Conditions URL | https://your-domain.example.com/terms |
| Opt-In Keywords | e.g. START, YES, JOIN |
| Contains Age Gated Content | No |

**Use Case Description (template):**
> [Your product name] is an AI-powered voice and messaging assistant for
> service businesses. We send SMS messages to customers on behalf of these
> businesses for appointment confirmations, reminders, waitlist
> notifications, follow-up scheduling, and customer care responses.
> Recipients opt in by providing their phone number when booking an
> appointment through the business's website or by calling the business
> directly.

**Sample Message:**
> Hi [Name], your appointment at [Business Name] is confirmed for [Date] at
> [Time]. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.

**Opt-In Confirmation Message:**
> You're confirmed! You'll receive appointment updates from [Business Name]
> via [Your Product Name]. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

**Help Message Sample:**
> [Your Product Name]: For help, contact [Business Name] directly or visit
> [your-domain.example.com]. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

**Additional Information (template):**
> Appointment booking, waitlist notifications, and customer care messages
> sent on behalf of local service businesses using the [Your Product Name]
> AI assistant platform. It answers customer calls, books appointments,
> manages waitlists, and sends SMS follow-ups to help businesses reduce
> no-shows and fill cancellations.
